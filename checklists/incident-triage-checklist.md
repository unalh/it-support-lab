# Incident Triage Checklist

This checklist helps IT support personnel triage and document incidents systematically.

## Steps

- Record incident details: user report, date/time, error messages, system logs.
- Determine scope and severity: affected systems, number of users impacted, business impact.
- Assign priority and create ticket in incident tracking system.
- Collect logs and reproduce the issue where possible.
- Provide first-line troubleshooting: check network connectivity, restart services, apply known fixes.
- If unresolved, document steps taken and gather additional information from user.
- Escalate to appropriate team or specialist if necessary.
- Communicate status updates to stakeholders.
- Close ticket with summary of resolution and update knowledge base.
